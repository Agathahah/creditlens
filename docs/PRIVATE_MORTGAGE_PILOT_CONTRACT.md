# Private mortgage pilot contract and admission boundary

20 October 2026. Current source access remains Freddie samples for private retrospective
research. No operational servicing source, operator usage agreement or authentic availability/
correction feed is available. Operational pilot admission is therefore NOT_ADMITTED; this
leaves the accepted v4 candidate and consumed tests unchanged.

## Intended business workflow

For a named mortgage operator, rank eligible active/current loans for analyst review against
the fixed three-month90+orREO research target. Scores alone do not authorize loan approval,
rejection, pricing, customer contact, collections or an automated credit action. A future
operator must define review capacity, permitted action, response window and outcome capture.
No loss reduction, operational recall, service SLO or lender/regulatory acceptance is claimed.
Mortgage results cannot be transferred to unsecured fintech loans without a new population
and evaluation contract. Historical LendingClub remains a separate rejected-model study.

## Admission sequence

1. Record provider/operator, product, population, rights, users and intended action. Link the
   concrete source agreement and metadata privately; a boolean assertion is not evidence.
2. Receive a minimal private schema/metadata sample before any bulk ingestion. Require stable
   pseudonymous loan key, monthly period, first-available time, correction/version identity,
   received time, status/delinquency, balance, termination and origination field availability.
3. Validate immutable source hashes, schema/types/units, keys/revisions, calendar gaps,
   availability versus prediction time and label maturity. Do not invent original availability
   from extraction time or overwrite corrections. Quarantine disagreements rather than score.
4. Lock pilot environment, eligibility, workload, review capacity, permitted actions, observation
   duration, security/retention/recovery and acceptance/stop criteria before receiving pilot
   outcome statistics. No re-selection on consumed2020/2022tests or monitoring observations.
5. Run a private shadow phase without credit actions. Preserve all eligible/excluded cases,
   actual request identities, versioned feature provenance and analyst/output consumption.
6. Join only outcomes available by the report cutoff. Unknown/unobserved/terminated cases remain
   explicit. Quality uncertainty must account for repeated loan-months and sample support.
   Positive events may resolve earlier; non-events require complete three-month follow-up.
7. Review actual errors, coverage, subgroup support, calibration, analyst-capacity ranking,
   operational failures and response records against the pre-agreed contract. Only a named
   release decision can admit a bounded operating version/environment/use. No automatic promote.
